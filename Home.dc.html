ALL QUESTIONS →
What exactly is a public adjuster? +

A state-licensed claims professional who represents you — never the insurance company. Besides an attorney, a public adjuster is the only person legally allowed to handle your claim on your behalf.

What does it cost me? +

Nothing up front, ever. We work on contingency — a small percentage of the settlement we win. If your claim isn't paid, our service is free. That's the guarantee.

My claim was already denied. Too late? +

Good — that's our specialty. A denial is the carrier's opening position, not the final word. We re-open, re-document and re-argue denied claims every week.
