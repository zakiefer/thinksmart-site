Four ways we fight.

Whatever stage your claim is at — not yet filed, stalled, shortchanged or flat-out denied — there's a way in. Pick yours.

S/01 — NEW CLAIMS

Filed right the first time.

The single biggest mistake homeowners make is filing alone. What you say in the first call, what gets documented in the first week — that's the claim. We take over before the carrier frames the story.

  • →Full-property inspection before the carrier's adjuster arrives
  • →Every conversation with the insurer handled for you
  • →Deadlines, notices and policy conditions — met to the letter

S/02 — DENIED CLAIMS

"No" is where we start.

Denials count on you giving up. We re-open the file, tear down the carrier's reasoning line by line, and rebuild the claim with evidence they can't wave away. Getting denied claims overturned is our specialty.

  • →Forensic review of the denial letter and your policy language
  • →New inspection, new documentation, new demand
  • →Escalation until the answer changes
70% of the denied claims we take on are overturned and paid.

S/03 — ESTIMATES

Numbers they can't shrink.

Carriers run your loss through pricing software tuned to their favor. We build independent, line-item estimates from the actual damage — measured, photographed and priced to real-world repair costs.

  • →Room-by-room, trade-by-trade scope of loss
  • →Code upgrades and hidden damage included — not "overlooked"
  • →Defensible in negotiation, appraisal or litigation
